Albert Einstein developed the theory of **special relativity**. It explains how
time and space are connected. Time and space can change when objects move very
fast.

Light always moves at the same speed. Because of this, time and space may need to
stretch or shrink depending on how fast you're moving compared to something else.

This theory explains [time dilation](/pages/time-dilation "Time Dilation"),
which is an important part of the relativistic Doppler effect.
