## What is Redshift?
a **redshift** is a decrease in the frequency of light. This can be caused by
the [relativistic Doppler effect](/ "Relativistic Doppler Effect"). The motion
of the source of a wave compared to us can change the frequency of the light.
The frequency of light is what determines its colour, and so a decrease in
frequency (redshift), will result in light that is more red. This is because red
light has the lowest frequency on the visible light spectrum.

![](/colour-spectrum.png "Colour Spectrum")

The image is the colour spectrum, red is on the left because it has
the lowest frequency. If any light is redshifted, it will move left on the
spectrum and become closer to red.

## What does it mean?
Because of the relativistic Doppler effect, we know that if the frequency of a
wave is decreasing (redshifting), it is because the thing making it is moving
away from us very fast. Astronomers have observed that the light waves that
reach us from far away galaxies is becoming more and more red. This means that
these galaxies must all be moving away from us very fast, and also that they are
speeding up. The fact that all of these galaxies are moving away from us is
evidence that the universe is expanding.
