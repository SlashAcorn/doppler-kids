## What is Redshift?
A **redshift** is a decrease in the frequency of light. This can be caused by
the [relativistic Doppler effect](/pages/relativistic-doppler-effect
"Relativistic Doppler Effect").

The motion of the light's source compared to us can change its frequency. \
The frequency of light determines its colour. So a decrease in frequency
(redshift) makes light appear more red. \
This is because red light has the lowest frequency on the visible light
spectrum.

{{< figure

This is the colour spectrum. Red is on the left because it has the lowest
frequency. Blue and violet are on the right because they have the highest
frequency.

If any light is redshifted, it will move left on the spectrum and
become closer to red. \
Light can also be blueshifted. This is where its frequency increases and so the
colour moves right on the spectrum and closer to blue.

## What does it mean?
{{< figure
    src="/doppler-kids/redshift.png"
    alt="diagram of stars moving towards, stationary, and away from earth and their change in colour"
    caption="Redshift"
    class="centred-figure-landscape"
>}}

Because of the relativistic Doppler effect, we know that if the frequency of a
wave is decreasing (redshifting), it is because the thing making it is moving
away from us very fast.

Astronomers have observed that the light waves that reach us from far away
galaxies are becoming more and more red. \
This means that these galaxies must all be moving away from us very fast.

The fact that all of these galaxies are moving away from us is evidence that the
universe is expanding.
