**Time dilation** is where time passes differently for things that are moving at
different speeds due to [special relativity](/pages/special-relativity "Special
Relativity"). When things move very fast, they appear to slow down. A clock that
is moving past you ticks more slowly ticks more slowly than a clock sitting
still next to you.
