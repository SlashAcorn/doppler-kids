## What is the Doppler Effect
The classical **Doppler effect** is when the thing making a wave (the source)
moves compared to the thing that sees or hears it (its
[observer](/pages/reference-frame "Reference Frame")). This changes the
[properties of the wave](/pages/waves "Waves").

In simple terms, a moving object can make different waves from an object that is
standing still.

## Understanding the Doppler Effect
First you need to understand how waves move.

Waves do not copy any movement from the thing that created them. \
They start at the point that they were made and ripple outwards. They are always
the same, whether their source was moving or not.

#### Car Simulation
{{< car-wave-sim >}}

Each wave starts from the spot that the car was when it made that wave, just
like when you were clicking the mouse.

Look at the circles in front of the car, the lines are closer together. Behind
the car, they are further apart.

This is the Doppler effect. \
As the car moves, it makes each new wave close to the edge of the last one. \
This squashes the waves together at the front.

The lines show the peaks (highest points) of the waves. A shorter gap between
the lines means a shorter wavelength. \
A shorter wavelength means a higher frequency, which is how many waves reach you
each second.

If you are in front of the car, you will hear a higher pitched sound (higher
frequency). Likewise, if you are behind the car you will hear a lower pitched
sound (lower frequency). \
See the graph of the wave being stretched out or squashed depending on
whether you are in front of or behind the source of the wave when it is moving.

You have probably heard the Doppler effect when a car drives past you.

The sound gets higher pitched as the car comes towards you, then goes lower as
it goes past. \
When the car is coming towards you, the gaps between the waves are small. When
the car passes, you are suddenly behind it, so the gaps get bigger.


You can hear this change in the video. The F1 car goes past very fast, so you go
from being in front of it to behind it very quickly. That is why the difference
in pitch of the car is so easy to hear.
