To refresh on what the relativistic Doppler effect actually is: \
It is when the source of light moves towards you or away from you very fast, the
colour of the light changes.

## Understanding the Relativistic Doppler Effect
The **relativistic Doppler effect** causes [Waves](/pages/waves "Waves") to be
squashed together and stretched apart.

This changes the colour of the light. The colour of light depends on its wave
frequency. \
This change is similar to how the [classical Doppler
effect](/pages/classical-doppler-effect "Classical Doppler Effect") changes
other types of waves, such as sound waves.

The thing that makes the relativistic Doppler effect different is that the
change in the wave is caused by [time dilation](/pages/time-dilation "Time
Dilation")on its own. Sometimes, it is caused by a changing distance between you
and the thing you are looking at (classical Doppler effect), added to the change
caused by time dilation

## Types of Relativistic Doppler Effect
There are two types of relativistic Doppler effect, transverse and longitudinal.
Consider the two cases in the diagram below.

![Diagram of space with the Earth orbited by a satellite and a space shuttle
flying away from the Earth](/space.png "Space Diagram ")

The satellite is an example of the transverse effect. The satellite orbits the
earth and communicates with us using light waves.

The spaceship is an example of the longitudinal effect. The spaceship is flying
away from the Earth, and is sending light waves back to the Earth.

### Transverse
In this case, the satellite orbits the Earth.

This means that its distance from the Earth doesn't change. It isn't
moving closer or further away, so the classical Doppler effect doesn't apply.

But the wave signals that we receive from satellites still appear to slow down.

See how the faster the satellite moves, the less frequently it makes a wave. \
This is because of time dilation

Because the satellite is moving much faster than us, we see its time slow down
due to time dilation. This means that we watch it produce its waves more slowly.
So fewer waves reach us each second, which means the frequency of the wave
decreases.

### Longitudinal
In this case, the ship is moving away from the Earth at a very high speed. \
This means that both time dilation and the classical Doppler effect apply to it.

Similarly to the satellite, the speed of the ship means that its time appears to
slow down. \
Because of time dilation, the ship appears to produce waves more slowly. This
makes the frequency drop.

But because the distance between the Earth and the ship is changing, the
classical Doppler effect also changes the wave frequency.

Just like for the car in the classical Doppler effect, because we are behind the
spaceship, the gaps in the waves get bigger. This means that the frequency
becomes lower.

Both of these effects add to each other to decrease the frequency of the wave
even more.

## What does this mean?
The relativistic Doppler effect explains that the universe is expanding. This is
because astronomers have observed [redshifting](/pages/redshift "Redshift").
