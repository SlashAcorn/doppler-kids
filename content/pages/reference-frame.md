title = 'Reference Frames'
+++

A **reference frame** is a perspective that you can view an event from. An event
can be anything, any action that happens can be an event, like a lightning
strike, or someone sitting in a chair. A reference frame is just the word given
to a point of view that you can see or observe that event from.

It is important to understand **reference frames** when thinking about
relativity because the way that you see an event can change depending on how you
look at it (which **reference frame** you use). You must consider who is the
observer, where are they, how are they moving, and how will that influence their
observation.
