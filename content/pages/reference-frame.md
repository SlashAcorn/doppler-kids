+++

A **reference frame** is a spot that you can view an event from. The spot can be
still, or moving. An event can be anything, like a lightning strike, or someone
sitting in a chair. A reference frame is just the word given to a point of view
that you can see (or observe) that event from.

It is important to understand **reference frames** when thinking about [special
relativity](/pages/special-relativity "Special Relativity"). The way that you
see an event changes depending on where you are and whether you are moving. You
must consider who the observer is, where they are and how they are moving, as
that can change what they see.
