## What is the Relativistic Doppler Effect
The **relativistic Doppler** effect describes how light changes. When the source
of the light and the [observer](/pages/reference-frame "Reference Frame") of the
light are moving towards or away from each other. If they move fast enough
(closer to the speed of light), [relativistic effects](/pages/special-relativity
"Special Relativity") happen. This makes the colour of the light appear to
change.
